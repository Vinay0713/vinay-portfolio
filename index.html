03 — Process

How a project runs

Same four steps every time, so you always know what happens next.

  1. STEP 01

    Scope call

    We go through what you need and what success looks like. Free, usually 20–30 minutes.

  2. STEP 02

    Written proposal

    Deliverables, milestones, price and delivery date in writing before any work starts.

  3. STEP 03

    Build in milestones

    You see working progress at each milestone and can redirect before the next one begins.

  4. STEP 04

    Handover

    Source code, credentials, a short walkthrough and 14 days of fixes on what was delivered.

04 — Working with me

What you can count on
